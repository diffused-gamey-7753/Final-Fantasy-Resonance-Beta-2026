## 🕹️ About

FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE is a single-player HD-2D turn-based RPG developed by Square Enix and LANCARSE, and published by Square Enix. Journey through a classic fantasy world of crystals, airships, espers, and moogles while uncovering a threat capable of destroying everything. The game launches October 22, 2026, and this beta test gives selected players an opportunity to play early, share feedback, and receive a free game key.

FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE is for fans of story-rich JRPGs, tactical turn-based battles, pixel-art adventures, and classic fantasy exploration.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- ⚔️ **Strategic Turn-Based Combat** — extension enemy weaknesses, stagger foes, seize extra turns, and unleash Resonance attacks.
- ✨ **Vision System** — Customize your party with Visions inspired by legendary FINAL FANTASY characters.
- 🎨 **Cinematic HD-2D** — Experience pixel-art characters, dynamic camera angles, and detailed 3D environments.
- 🌍 **Classic Fantasy World** — Explore a world filled with crystals, airships, espers, moogles, chocobos, and familiar legends.
- 🏆 **Optional Challenges** — Discover side content including the Colosseum, Chamber of Arms, Gilgamesh, and Ultima Weapon.
